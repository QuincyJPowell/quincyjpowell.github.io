# Seven Oaks Farm

Explore the acreage of the run-down Seven Oaks Farm! Sow seeds, grow crops, care for animals, and make your farm a success!

With a wide variety of crops, animals, townsfolk, travelers, and more, boredom will be the last thing you experience!

Featuring hand-drawn pixel art, Seven Oaks Farm will be your next big gaming addiction. Right up until things get weird, that is. Mysteries abound! Get every ending to figure out the truth!

---

## Latest Progress

I’m currently in the worldbuilding phase of development. I’m planning out all of the assets I’ll need, the background I’ll need to know as the developer, and the story that will unfold throughout the game.

At least three major endings are already planned, with at least two dozen NPCs. I’m planning for a large variety of crops and animals. There is quite a lot to juggle in this planning phase, so it will take a bit of time.

Once I have a game plan laid out, I’ll start in on the art design! I’ll first finalize the scale, then I can begin making asset models!

I’m not sure yet what my goal will be for a demo release. I suppose I’ll have to wait and see as I develop things!
