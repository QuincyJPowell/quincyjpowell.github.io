# Website Development

The construction of my personal website is, first and foremost, a labor of love. It encapsulates my professional and academic portfolios, both of which I am continually working to develop, and provides a home for my personal projects.

The site was developed and is hosted through GitHub. The coding, site construction, and troubleshooting were all part of the basic development process. A mixture of coding languages are used, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

My website will continually be under development as I add new projects and refine existing content.
