# Progenitor

Emmi Hawk is an expert in her field.

Humanity is scrambling, squeezing into increasingly smaller bunkers for protection. Protection from what? The end of the world.

Horrific creatures, scientific inventions engineered to be the most effective biological weapons have spread everywhere. Apex predators, Apexes. Hunting humanity near to the point of collapse, there seems to be no solution.

Hawk is one of the brave individuals who regularly stand face-to-face with the Apexes, one of the even fewer who continues to survive. And as the singular daughter of the man in charge of Project APX, she feels responsible for saving what’s left of the world before it completely breaks down from the unyielding pressure of the Apexes.
